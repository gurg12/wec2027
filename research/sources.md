## 14. Historical base rates
- Stagflation/interregnum episodes: ~4–17 yrs (Great Inflation 1965–82; acute 1968–82), ended by policy regime-break (Volcker: FF to ~20%, CPI <4% by 1983 at ~10% unemployment).
- GPT diffusion lag: David (1990) dynamo paper — ~30–40 yrs from commercial dynamo to measured TFP (1920s); complements, not tech, are the constraint.
- Debt: Reinhart–Rogoff 90% "cliff" discredited (Herndon-Ash-Pollin); treat >110% debt/GDP as drag multiplier, not deterministic trigger.
- Great-power war: zero direct since 1945 (~80 yrs); one model puts ~21% odds of that streak by chance — low-but-nonzero prior justified.
- Cyber: largest single event ever ~$10bn (NotPetya 2017); a >$100bn event has **zero** historical occurrences — 2034's event is a first-of-kind tail, and the text should acknowledge that.
