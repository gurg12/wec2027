## Topic 1: Gold and precious metals

### 2025 rally
- Gold rose **+64% over calendar 2025**, its largest annual gain since 1979.
  Source: https://goldsilver.com/industry-news/article/gold-price-forecast-2026-2027-key-predictions-from-top-analysts/
- Central banks bought **863 tonnes of gold in 2025** (another source rounds to ~850t, third-highest year on record); WGC notes an average of **~1,000t/year since 2022**.
  Sources: https://goldsilver.com/industry-news/article/gold-price-forecast-2026-2027-key-predictions-from-top-analysts/ ; https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-mid-year-outlook-2026

### January 2026 spike
- Spot gold pierced **$5,000/oz for the first time**, then peaked at **$5,589.38 intraday on 28 January 2026**; LBMA Gold Price PM fix high of **$5,405.00 on 29 January**; 12 all-time highs set in the month; gold gained **~29.5% in January 2026 alone** (Kitco).
  Sources: https://goldsilver.com/industry-news/article/gold-price-forecast-2026-2027-key-predictions-from-top-analysts/ ; https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-mid-year-outlook-2026 ; https://gfolio.in/gold-silver-correction-july-2026-healthy-or-end-of-rally/
- Spike drivers: geopolitical risk premium from the **Iran conflict / Strait of Hormuz disruption** (Brent >$90/bbl), Fed easing expectations, record ETF inflows, elevated options activity.
  Source: https://www.moneymagpie.com/investment-articles/gold-price-prediction-july-2026

### Correction and current price (July 2026)
- Gold corrected **~22–25% from the January peak**; trading **~$4,000–4,100/oz** entering July 2026 (Comex ~$4,026 on 2 July; WGC cites ~$4,100 as of 26 June, ~-7% YTD). Still up ~70% vs. January 2025.
  Sources: https://gfolio.in/gold-silver-correction-july-2026-healthy-or-end-of-rally/ ; https://www.gold.org/goldhub/research/gold-mid-year-outlook-2026
- Correction drivers: US–Iran diplomacy in Doha (late June 2026) took Brent back below $80; US 10-yr Treasury yields rose to **~4.46–4.5%**; fading ETF inflows.
  Source: https://gfolio.in/gold-silver-correction-july-2026-healthy-or-end-of-rally/ ; https://www.moneymagpie.com/investment-articles/gold-price-prediction-july-2026

  Sources: https://gfolio.in/gold-silver-correction-july-2026-healthy-or-end-of-rally/ ; https://www.bestbrokers.com/gold-brokers/countries-gold-reserves/

**Constraint note (30-yr scenario, H2 2026 start):** The scenario must open with gold ~$4,000–4,100 (not at the $5,589 peak), a demonstrated regime of geopolitically-driven 20–30% spikes and corrections, structurally elevated CB demand (~750–1,000t/yr de-dollarization bid) as a persistent floor, and a wide credible forecast band ($4,900–$6,300 for end-2026/27) — i.e., long-run paths should treat gold as structurally repriced upward but violently cyclical, with silver ~1.5–2x gold's beta.

## Topic 2: Russia–Ukraine as of mid-2026

### Battlefield / front line
- Russia captured **Pokrovsk and Myrnohrad in early 2026** (entered Pokrovsk Nov 2025) but has made **no significant advances west of them since December 2025**; fighting concentrated around Kostiantynivka and north/west of Pokrovsk. On 2 July 2026 alone, 28 Russian attacks in the Pokrovsk direction with **no confirmed territorial gains**. Overall picture: Russia can still attack but cannot convert tactical gains into operational success; front largely static/grinding.
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pokrovsk_offensive ; https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/world/war/russo-ukraine-2626-07-02.htm ; https://www.lvivherald.com/post/the-ukrainian-front-line-in-june-2026

### Ukrainian deep-strike campaign
- Russian refineries hit **at least 194 times in H1 2026** — an **11-fold increase** over H1 2025; record **16 successful refinery strikes in May 2026**; **11 refineries + 8 defense-industry plants struck in June 2026** (Ukraine MoD).
  Sources: https://united24media.com/war-in-ukraine/ukraine-hit-russian-oil-refineries-nearly-200-times-in-first-half-of-2026-20462 ; https://mod.gov.ua/en/news/ukraine-struck-11-russian-oil-refineries-and-8-defense-industry-facilities-results-of-june-deep-strike-operations
- Refinery outage share: Carnegie (Vakulenko) estimates **13–17% real-time effective throughput loss**; the widely-cited **38–40% figure is cumulative capacity of every facility hit since 2024**, not simultaneous outage. IEA cut its 2026 Russian refining-throughput forecast by 150,000 bpd to **5.0 mb/d**, citing Ukrainian attacks.
  Sources: https://carnegieendowment.org/russia-eurasia/politika/2026/06/russia-new-refinery-strike ; https://www.aljazeera.com/opinions/2026/7/1/is-ukraines-campaign-of-targeting-russian-refineries-working
- Moscow strikes: largest Ukrainian drone attack on Moscow of the war hit the **Gazprom Neft Moscow refinery** (supplies ~40% of Moscow's fuel market, ~70% of the capital region's gasoline); Reuters-sourced reports say it is **unlikely to resume operation before 2027** (repairs ≥6 months). Fuel shortages affect **~60 Russian regions**, with rationing in Crimea, Sevastopol, Kursk, Moscow region, and St Petersburg.
  Sources: https://kyivindependent.com/moscow-oil-refinery-unlikely-to-resume-operations-this-year-after-ukrainian-drone-attacks/ ; https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/ukrainian-drones-set-a-moscow-refinery-ablaze-in-a-major-attack-on-the-russian-capital ; https://www.kyivpost.com/post/78886
- US targeting data reportedly assisted Ukrainian refinery strikes in 2026. Source: https://english.nv.ua/amp/u-s-data-assisted-ukrainian-drone-strikes-on-russian-refineries-in-2026-50622020.html

### Russian economic deterioration
- GDP growth: **~1% expected for 2026** (IMF: 0.6% in 2025, 1.0% in 2026); BOFIT sees a low-growth trajectory through 2026–28.
  Sources: https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/01/02/russias-economy-in-2026-more-war-slower-growth-and-higher-taxes-a91579 ; https://www.bofit.fi/en/forecasting/latest-forecast-for-russia/
- Inflation: **6.47% annual as of 19 Jan 2026**, expected to accelerate on a VAT hike; deficit spending constrains CBR rate cuts.
  Source: https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/01/02/russias-economy-in-2026-more-war-slower-growth-and-higher-taxes-a91579
- National Wealth Fund: liquid assets down from **6.5% of GDP pre-2022 to 1.8% of GDP at end-2025** (~4 trillion rubles); Finance Ministry selling yuan/gold at a record **12.8 bn rubles/day** — at that pace, liquid assets largely exhausted by end-2026.
  Source: https://theins.ru/en/economics/289363
- Banking stress: state banks are the marginal buyer of government debt — **Sberbank alone holds ~25% of outstanding OFZ federal bonds**; 2023–24 consumer/banking liquidity boom over.
  Source: https://thinktank.4freerussia.org/economics/russias-budget-crisis-explained/

### Peace process (28-point / Witkoff plan)
- The **28-point Witkoff–Dmitriev plan** leaked November 2025 (seen as heavily pro-Moscow); revised into a **20-point Ukraine–US framework unveiled by Zelensky on 24 Dec 2025**. Three rounds of US–Ukraine–Russia talks (UAE and Switzerland, late Jan–Feb 2026) produced **no breakthrough**; early-March 2026 UAE round **postponed due to US/Israeli military action against Iran**. Sticking points: territorial cession in Donbas and control of the Zaporizhzhia NPP; Russia showing little willingness to compromise. Status mid-2026: **stalled**.
  Sources: https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10411/ ; https://kyivindependent.com/zelensky-reveals-20-point-peace-plan-draft-backed-by-ukraine-us/ ; https://www.csis.org/analysis/unfinished-plan-peace-ukraine-provision-provision ; https://time.com/7338224/trump-ukraine-russia-putin-peace-negotiations/

### EU €90bn package
- **€90 billion EU loan for Ukraine for 2026–27**, agreed by the European Council in December 2025, approved by the European Parliament in February 2026, finalised by the Council **23 April 2026**: **€30bn macro-financial/budget support** (via Ukraine Facility) + **€60bn for defence capabilities/military procurement**. Disbursements so far: **€3.2bn MFA on 25 June 2026** and **€3.9bn on 30 June 2026** (drone procurement) — €7.1bn total.
  Sources: https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20260206IPR33903/parliament-approves-EU90-billion-ukraine-support-loan-package ; https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/04/23/council-finalises-90-billion-support-loan-to-ukraine/ ; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/23/eu-formally-approves-90bn-euro-ukraine-loan-and-new-sanctions-on-russia

### Putin age / succession chatter
- Putin is **73** (turns 74 on 7 Oct 2026). Succession commentary is active in 2026 think-tank output (FPRI "Russia After Putin" Apr 2026, Cipher Brief scenarios, RAND); Khodorkovsky's framing circulating: "All our dictators quit by 80. Putin is 73." Expert consensus: he most likely dies in office; no anointed successor. Health rumors (Parkinson's, body doubles) remain **UNVERIFIED and mostly debunked over the past decade**. Note: **Sergei Ivanov**, once seen as a possible successor, **died in 2026 at 73**.
  Sources: https://khodorkovsky.com/russias-succession-fear-all-our-dictators-quit-by-80-putin-is-73/ ; https://www.fpri.org/article/2026/04/russia-after-putin/ ; https://www.kyivpost.com/post/79074 ; https://www.rand.org/pubs/commentary/2025/10/preparing-for-the-death-of-putin.html

**Constraint note (30-yr scenario, H2 2026 start):** The scenario must open with an ongoing, largely static attritional war (no peace deal — the 28→20-point track is stalled as of mid-2026), Ukraine holding escalation dominance in deep strikes (13–17% of Russian refining offline, ~60 regions in fuel rationing), Russia fiscally squeezed but not collapsing (~1% growth, NWF liquid reserves near exhaustion by end-2026), Europe committed through 2027 (€90bn), and a 73-year-old Putin with unresolved succession — so plausible branch points are (a) a frozen-conflict deal in 2026–27, (b) Russian fiscal/leadership rupture later in the decade, not a near-term Russian battlefield victory.
