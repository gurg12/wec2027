## 1. Demographics

### UN WPP 2024 baseline and the undershoot pattern
- UN World Population Prospects 2024 (latest revision as of mid-2026; next revision expected 2026-2027): world population ~8.2bn (2024), median projection peaks at **~10.3bn in 2084**, declining to ~10.2bn by 2100 — peak revised down and earlier vs WPP 2022 (10.4bn in 2086), partly because fertility in the largest countries came in below prior assumptions (China revised to TFR ~1.01). Sources: https://population.un.org/wpp/ ; https://populationconnection.org/blog/new-un-population-projections-where-are-we-headed/
- Global TFR: **2.25 (2024 estimate; often quoted ~2.2)**, projected to hit replacement (2.1) around **2036**. Source: https://populationconnection.org/blog/new-un-population-projections-where-are-we-headed/ ; https://www.un.org/development/desa/pd/sites/www.un.org.development.desa.pd/files/undesa_pd_2025_wfr_2024_final.pdf (UN "World Fertility 2024")
- Registered-births-undershoot pattern: confirmed at country level (see below — Japan's 2025 births arrived **~15 years earlier than the 2023 official projection**; China TFR ~1.0 vs earlier UN assumptions of stabilization). A single global "registered births vs WPP 2024" reconciliation for 2024-25 was not found. **UNVERIFIED as a single global statistic** — supported instead by country-level evidence that actual births keep coming in below projections in East Asia, Europe, and the US.
- Alternative low projection: IHME (Lancet, Vollset et al. 2020) reference scenario peaks at **~9.73bn in 2064**, falling to ~8.79bn by 2100 — i.e., ~20 years earlier and ~0.6bn lower than UN median. Source: https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(20)30677-2/fulltext (pre-2025 publication; no newer IHME full revision found in searches).

### Country data (latest actuals)
- **China**: ~8.7 million births estimated for 2025 (vs 23.1M in India); TFR ~1.0 or lower. Sources: https://www.visualcapitalist.com/global-births-by-country-in-2025/ ; https://www.visualcapitalist.com/mapped-every-countrys-fertility-rate-births-decline/
- **Japan**: **705,809 births in 2025** — 10th consecutive annual decline, lowest since records began in 1899; reached this level **>15 years earlier than the 2023 IPSS projection**; TFR at record low. Sources: https://japantoday.com/category/national/births-in-japan-fall-in-2025-to-706-000-record-low-for-10th-straight-year ; https://www.nippon.com/en/japan-data/h02717/
- **South Korea**: **254,500 births in 2025**, up 16,100 (+6.4%) vs 2024 — second consecutive annual rise, largest increase since 2010; TFR rebounded to ~0.8 (from 0.72-0.75 trough). Durability of rebound uncertain. Sources: https://www.asianews.it/news-en/In-2025,-births-in-Japan-continued-to-decline,-while-in-South-Korea,-they-rose-for-a-second-year-64929.html ; https://worldpopulationreview.com/country-rankings/total-fertility-rate
- **Italy**: TFR ~1.2 (2023, record low); crude birth rate below 7/1,000; Europe total births only ~6.3M in 2025. Exact Italy 2025 birth count (~370k per Istat trend) **UNVERIFIED** — searches confirmed TFR and sub-7/1,000 birth rate only. Sources: https://www.visualcapitalist.com/mapped-global-birth-rates-around-the-world-2025/ ; https://www.visualcapitalist.com/global-births-by-country-in-2025/
- **US**: TFR ~1.58 in 2025 (down from 1.59 in 2024); total births roughly flat to slightly down y/y (~3.6M range). Source: https://www.newsweek.com/worldwide-birth-rate-2025-2094691 ; https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/08/15/5-facts-about-global-fertility-trends/
- **Africa**: sub-Saharan Africa projected to grow ~3x, from **~1.2bn today to ~3.4bn by 2100** (UN WPP 2024) — the only major region with a still-expanding youth bulge; global births ~132M/yr, with an increasing share African. Precise Africa youth-cohort (15-24) counts for 2050 **UNVERIFIED in this pass** — commonly cited: ~1 in 4 humans will be African by 2050. Source: https://populationconnection.org/blog/new-un-population-projections-where-are-we-headed/

**Constraint note:** Any 30-year scenario from H2 2026 must have East Asia and Southern Europe in accelerating workforce decline (the workers of 2050 are already born), a US kept near-flat only by immigration, an African labor-supply boom as the sole large youth source, and should treat UN medium-variant population as a soft ceiling — actual births keep undershooting it, so the realistic corridor lies between UN median and IHME-low.

## 2. Energy transition

### Solar and battery learning curves
- Global solar installs 2025: record year, but estimates diverge by methodology — **SolarPower Europe: 664 GW** (global fleet passed 3 TW); IEA: 605 GW; PV Tech/consultancies: ~647 GW; IRENA (capacity-added accounting): 511 GW. China alone: **382 GW (57% of global)**; India #2 at 45.7 GW, passing the US. Use "~600-665 GW, China >55%". Sources: https://www.solarpowereurope.org/press-releases/new-report-global-solar-market-hits-new-record-of-664-gw-installation-in-2025-as-global-solar-fleet-passes-3-tw ; https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/04/21/the-world-added-605-gw-of-new-pv-capacity-in-2025-says-iea/ ; https://pv-magazine-usa.com/2026/04/02/global-solar-additions-reached-record-511-gw-in-2025-says-irena/
- Module prices: US median **~$0.28/W** (Q1 2026, inflated by tariffs/FEOC rules and flat-to-up vs 2025); global/China spot modules remain far cheaper (roughly $0.09-0.12/W per pv-magazine indices — **UNVERIFIED exact global spot number in this pass**). Source: https://pv-magazine-usa.com/2026/04/03/u-s-solar-module-prices-face-upward-pressure-as-trade-risks-and-feoc-rules-dominate-q1-2026/
- Batteries: BNEF global average li-ion **pack price $108/kWh in 2025** (record low, -8% y/y), forecast ~$105/kWh in 2026; LFP cells ~$95-110/kWh; US utility-scale BESS installed cost ~$330-420/kWh; US residential installed ~$850-1,050/kWh. Sources: https://www.energy-storage.news/li-ion-battery-pack-prices-fell-8-since-last-year-despite-metals-prices-rising-bloombergnef-says/ ; https://www.sunwaypv.com/blog/how-much-does-a-battery-energy-storage-system-cost/ ; https://www.energysage.com/energy-storage/how-much-do-batteries-cost/

### Demand growth and AI load
- IEA Electricity 2026: global electricity demand to grow **~3.6%/yr average 2026-2030** (vs 2.8%/yr past decade) = **~1,100 TWh added per year** (vs ~700 TWh/yr 2015-2025).
- Data centers: **485 TWh (2025) → ~950 TWh (2030)**, ~3% of global demand by 2030; AI-focused data-center consumption grew ~50% in 2025 and is projected to **triple 2025→2030**; data centers = ~50% of US demand growth to 2030 (US adds >420 TWh over five years). Sources: https://www.iea.org/reports/electricity-2026/executive-summary ; https://www.iea.org/reports/electricity-2026/demand ; https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary

### Grid bottlenecks
- US interconnection queue (LBNL "Queued Up" 2025-26 data): **~8,200 active projects, ~1,312 GW generation + ~749 GW storage** (~2,000+ GW total; some 2026 sources cite ~2,600 GW backlog). Median request-to-commercial-operation now **>5 years**. Notable 2025 shift: active gas capacity +86% (to 253 GW) while solar (-19%), storage (-16%), wind (-19%) shrank in the queue. Sources: https://emp.lbl.gov/queues ; https://www.osti.gov/biblio/3008763 ; https://enkiai.com/ai-market-intelligence/grid-interconnection-delays-2026-a-threat-to-us-energy/
- Transformer lead times: high-voltage transformers repeatedly cited in 2026 as the binding physical bottleneck (with permitting) after study reforms; specific figure (commonly 3-5 years for large power transformers) **UNVERIFIED as a precise 2026 number** — sources confirm equipment lead times are a primary constraint. Source: https://www.eckertseamans.com/legal-updates/interconnection-risk-in-2026-how-grid-congestion-ai-load-growth-and-queue-delays-are-impacting-renewable-energy-development

### Fusion and SMRs
- Commonwealth Fusion Systems SPARC: assembly under way; **first plasma slipped to ~2027** (from 2025 target; late-2026/early-2027 per some statements); digital-twin partnership with Siemens/Nvidia (CES 2026), Google DeepMind TORAX plasma-optimization deal (Sept 2025). Q>1 not yet demonstrated. Sources: https://fortune.com/2026/01/07/fusion-power-commonwealth-sparc-nuclear-fusion-pilot-ai-siemens-nvidia/ ; https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/assembly-starts-of-sparc-as-iter-cryopumps-completed
- Helion: still targeting **50 MW to Microsoft by 2028** (Polaris net-electricity demonstration pending — widely viewed as aggressive). ITER: component milestones continuing (cryopumps complete); first plasma per 2024 rebaseline not before ~2034 (**UNVERIFIED in this pass**; searches only confirmed component progress). Source: https://pdpspectra.com/blog/fusion-energy-2026-commonwealth-helion/
- SMRs: first Western SMR under construction — **GE-Hitachi BWRX-300 at OPG Darlington (construction began May 2025, C$7.7bn/US$5.6bn for unit 1, online target 2030)**; NuScale 77 MW design NRC-approved, Doicesti (Romania) 462 MW FID expected as early as 2026; Hungary LOI for up to 10 BWRX-300s. Hyperscaler orders (Google-Kairos ~500 MW by 2035, Amazon-X-energy ~5 GW by 2039) reported in 2024 — **status as of 2026 UNVERIFIED in this pass**. Sources: https://neutronbytes.com/2025/05/17/geh-bwrx-300-smr-approved-for-construction-at-opgs-darlingtion-site/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BWRX-300

**Constraint note:** For a 30-year scenario, cheap solar+storage (~$100/kWh packs, >600 GW/yr installs, still on learning curves) is the default marginal electron, AI adds ~500 TWh of new load by 2030 alone, and the binding constraints are grid interconnection/transformers (5+ year queues) rather than generation cost — while fusion contributes nothing material before the late 2030s and SMRs only single-digit GW before ~2035.

## 3. Robotics

### Humanoid unit economics (2026)
- **Unitree**: G1 base ~$16,000 direct (~$17,990 on Amazon); EDU versions $43,900-73,900. Shipped **5,500+ units in 2025** — more than all other humanoid makers combined; targeting 10,000-20,000 units in 2026. AgiBot (China) sold 5,168 units in 2025. Sources: https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/04/27/unitree-g1-humanoid-robots-are-reshaping-the-robotics-investment-stack/ ; https://lumichats.com/blog/humanoid-robots-2026-tesla-optimus-figure-ai-unitree-complete-guide
- **Figure**: Figure 03 fleet of ~40 units deployed at BMW Spartanburg, billed ~**$25/robot-operating-hour** (RaaS model). Source: https://lumichats.com/blog/humanoid-robots-2026-tesla-optimus-figure-ai-unitree-complete-guide
- **Tesla Optimus**: only **hundreds built in 2025** (vs earlier "thousands" talk); current unit cost est. $50,000-100,000; long-run target price $20,000-30,000. Source: https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/humanoid-robotics-optimus-deployment-tracker
- **1X NEO**: $20,000 outright or $499/month (home humanoid, teleop-heavy). Source: https://theresarobotforthat.com/blog/humanoid-robot-companies-2026-complete-guide/
- Industry forecast: TrendForce sees global humanoid shipments >**50,000 units in 2026** (~+700% y/y) — i.e., total cumulative fleet still trivial vs ~350M-person global manufacturing workforce. Source: https://kraneshares.com/humanoid-robotics-in-2026-the-race-from-pilot-to-platform/

### China humanoid rental market (the tell)
- China robot rental market ~**1bn yuan (~$140M) in 2025**, projected ≥10bn yuan in 2026; **>153,000 robot-rental businesses** registered; standardized event rentals (weddings, exhibitions, retail stunts — e.g. Haidilao hotpot leasing robots to dance/write calligraphy for Spring Festival 2026). Sources: https://english.news.cn/20260210/82c1c9caa98047168b46d24e5db1667e/c.html ; https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202512/1351693.shtml (see also https://www.bjreview.com/China/202602/t20260209_800430019.html)
- CNN (2026-06-30): rental operators report frequent failures, limited autonomy, heavy reliance on remote human operators — "every deployment still comes bundled with a human engineer"; training farms have 120+ humanoids doing single repeated tasks under handheld-controller guidance to harvest physical-world data. Robots earn money as spectacle, not labor. Source: https://edition.cnn.com/2026/06/30/tech/china-humanoid-robot-ai-rental-intl-hnk-dst

### Warehouse automation
- Amazon passed **1,000,000 robots deployed** (mid-2025) across 300+ fulfillment centers — approaching parity with its ~1.5M human workforce; goal to automate **75% of fulfillment operations**; plans to replicate the Shreveport next-gen design in ~40 facilities by end-2027; internal projections: avoid hiring 160,000+ US workers by 2027, up to 600,000 positions replaced by 2033. DeepFleet AI traffic model cut robot travel time ~10%. Sources: https://www.forbes.com/sites/ronschmelzer/2025/07/07/amazons-millionth-warehouse-robot-is-here-and-its-getting-smarter/ ; https://www.metaintro.com/blog/amazon-1-million-robots-600000-warehouse-jobs-workers-2026

### Autonomous vehicles
- **Waymo**: **500,000 paid rides/week** (early 2026) across 10 US cities — 10x growth from 50k/week in May 2024; markets: Phoenix, SF, LA, Austin, Atlanta, Miami, Dallas, Houston, San Antonio, Orlando; 2026 additions planned: Washington DC, Detroit, Las Vegas, San Diego, Denver; target **1M rides/week by end-2026**; raised ~$16bn (Feb 2026); **London named first overseas market**, testing in Tokyo. Sources: https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/27/waymo-skyrocketing-ridership-in-one-chart/ ; https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/waymos-metric-for-2026-success-one-million-weekly-rides/
- **Tesla robotaxi**: paid service in Austin; opened unsupervised rides across the full Austin metro (~245 sq mi) on **2026-06-03**, but active fleet only **~20 vehicles**; no California permits for unsupervised ride-hail yet. Source: https://thechargeport.com/robotaxi-tracker
- **Europe**: no Madrid robotaxi launch found — **UNVERIFIED (Madrid)**. What exists instead: Uber + Pony.ai + Verne plan Europe's first robotaxi service starting in **Zagreb**, with more European cities to follow; Waymo's London entry announced. Source: https://thechargeport.com/robotaxi-tracker

**Constraint note:** As of H2 2026 the scenario must start from humanoids being cheap-ish hardware (~$16k) with no autonomous economic function (rental-as-spectacle, ~$25/hr RaaS pilots, <20k cumulative useful units), while purpose-built automation (Amazon's 1M robots) and driving autonomy (Waymo 500k rides/week, doubling yearly) are the proven scaling curves — so labor displacement over 30 years should be modeled as task-specific automation compounding now, with general-purpose humanoids as a mid-2030s-at-earliest wildcard.
