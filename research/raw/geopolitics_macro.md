# Grounding dossier: Geopolitics & Macro, as of 2026-07-07

Compiled via live web search on 2026-07-07. All facts below were verified against current sources unless marked UNVERIFIED. Purpose: ground a 30-year scenario document starting H2 2026.

## Topic 1 — The 2026 Iran war and the Islamabad Memorandum

### Timeline of the war
- **28 Feb 2026**: US and Israeli forces launched ~900 strikes in 12 hours on Iranian missiles, air defenses, military infrastructure, and leadership. Supreme Leader **Ali Khamenei was killed** in the initial wave, along with dozens of officials; a strike on a girls' school adjacent to a naval base in Minab (near Bandar Abbas) killed ~170 civilians.
  - Source: https://www.britannica.com/event/2026-Iran-war ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war
  - Constraint note: A US–Israel decapitation war against Iran in Feb 2026 is a fixed initial condition; any 30-year scenario must start from a shattered Iranian deterrent and a precedent for direct great-power strikes on a major oil state.
- **28 Feb 2026 onward — Strait of Hormuz crisis**: Iran retaliated with missile/drone strikes on US embassies, bases, and oil infrastructure, and largely blocked the Strait of Hormuz (~13 mb/d normally transits, ~1/5 of global oil consumption). The IEA characterized it as the **largest disruption in the history of the global oil market**, causing fuel shortages in parts of Asia.
  - Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_crisis ; https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=67424
  - Constraint note: The scenario world has already experienced a demonstrated full Hormuz closure — energy-security policy, SPR sizing, and Asian import diversification for the next 30 years will be shaped by this proof-of-concept shock.
- **21–30 Mar 2026**: Trump threatened Iran's civilian energy infrastructure, then Kharg Island (terminal for nearly all Iranian oil exports) and desalination plants, setting an April 7 deadline for reopening Hormuz.
  - Source: https://www.britannica.com/event/2026-Iran-war
- **7–8 Apr 2026**: US and Iran agreed to a **ceasefire that included Israel**, after more than five weeks of fighting.
  - Source: https://www.britannica.com/event/2026-Iran-war ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war_ceasefire
- **~12 Apr 2026**: First round of Islamabad peace talks **collapsed**; the US said it would **blockade Iranian ports**. Hostilities/pressure continued in some form well past the April ceasefire — Al Jazeera describes Pakistan mediating an agreement "after more than 100 days of war" (i.e., into mid-June).
  - Source: https://www.npr.org/2026/04/12/nx-s1-5782538/u-s-iran-peace-talks-islamabad-collapse ; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/15/how-pakistan-mediated-a-us-iran-agreement-after-more-than-100-days-of-war
  - Constraint note: The April ceasefire was fragile and broke down at least once — scenarios should not treat the June memorandum as a stable Westphalian settlement but as a truce with demonstrated failure modes.

### The Islamabad Memorandum
- **12 Jun 2026**: Pakistani PM Shehbaz Sharif announced US and Iran had agreed on final text. **14–15 Jun**: memorandum digitally signed by representatives (announced by VP JD Vance). **17 Jun 2026**: Trump signed remotely during dinner with Macron at Versailles (after the G7 summit); Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian then signed in Tehran.
  - Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad_Memorandum ; https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-peace-deal-agreement/
- Officially the "Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding between the United States of America and the Islamic Republic of Iran." Brokered primarily by **Pakistan** (team: PM Shehbaz Sharif, Field Marshal Asim Munir, FM Ishaq Dar), with Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and Egypt also facilitating.
  - Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad_Memorandum ; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/15/how-pakistan-mediated-a-us-iran-agreement-after-more-than-100-days-of-war
  - Constraint note: Pakistan's emergence as the decisive US–Iran broker (with Gulf states as junior facilitators) rewires South/West Asian diplomacy — a 30-year scenario should carry forward elevated Pakistani strategic relevance and US–Pakistan ties.
- **Provisions**: a **60-day extension of the ceasefire** to negotiate final terms (window runs roughly mid-June to mid-August 2026). It is a framework only: **no accord on Iran's nuclear program or uranium stockpiles**, though it calls for downgrading Iranian uranium from weapons-grade to reactor-grade following a final agreement. On the table for the 60-day talks: future of the Strait of Hormuz, Iran's frozen assets, long-term sanctions relief, and the nuclear program.
  - Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad_Memorandum ; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/1/us-iran-negotiations-whats-the-latest
  - Constraint note: The scenario's opening state has an unresolved Iranian nuclear question inside a ticking 60-day window ending ~mid-Aug 2026 — the first branch point of the 30-year tree is whether a final deal lands by then.

### Status of talks as of early July 2026
- **1 Jul 2026**: Indirect US–Iran meetings in **Doha** made "positive progress" on Islamabad-MoU issues (Vance: talks "going well"); next meeting to be scheduled "at the earliest possible time" after Khamenei funeral processions. Note: a Geneva round had been **abruptly postponed** around June 19.
  - Source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/01/world/live-news/iran-war-trump ; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/19/oil-prices-wti-brent-crude-us-iran-deal-strait-hormuz-shipping-recovery.html
- CFR describes the peace talks as having hit at least one **impasse**; Israeli strikes in Lebanon were reported as threatening the agreement around signing time.
  - Source: https://www.cfr.org/articles/u-s-iran-peace-talks-hit-an-impasse-what-comes-next ; https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-peace-deal-agreement/

### Succession: Mojtaba Khamenei
- **9 Mar 2026**: Iran's Assembly of Experts named **Mojtaba Khamenei** (son of Ali Khamenei) as the third supreme leader.
  - Source: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/8/iran-names-khameneis-son-as-new-supreme-leader-after-fathers-killing-2 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iranian_supreme_leader_election
- As of early July 2026 Mojtaba **has made no public appearance** since taking office; he communicates only via written statements (never shown his face or used his voice). US Defense Secretary Hegseth said in March he was "wounded and likely disfigured" in the strike that killed his father, mother, and wife; Reuters reported in April he was recovering from severe facial and leg injuries, possibly having lost a leg. Ali Khamenei's public funeral was held ~4–5 July 2026; Mojtaba's absence is fueling public insecurity and questions over who actually governs.
  - Source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/05/world/live-news/iran-khamenei-funeral-war-trump ; https://time.com/article/2026/07/05/khamenei-s-funeral-is-meant-to-project-strength-but-iran-s-new-leader-has-yet-to-appear/ ; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/6/khamenei-family-mourns-but-mojtabas-absence-fuels-public-insecurity
  - Constraint note: Iran enters H2 2026 with an invisible, possibly incapacitated supreme leader — regime-stability, IRGC-power, and succession-crisis branches are live from day one of the scenario.
- **UNVERIFIED — Khamenei funeral timing oddity**: the public funeral occurred in early July 2026, ~4 months after the Feb 28 killing. Searches confirmed the funeral dates (July 4–5 per CNN live blogs) but I did not verify why it was delayed (presumably wartime conditions); treat the delay explanation as unconfirmed.
- **UNVERIFIED — casualty totals**: a Wikipedia page "Casualties of the 2026 Iran war" exists but I did not fetch aggregate figures; only the Minab school strike (~170 dead) is confirmed here.

## Topic 2 — Global macro baseline, mid-2026

### IMF World Economic Outlook, April 2026 ("Global Economy in the Shadow of War", published 14 Apr 2026)
- **Global growth**: projected to slow to **3.1% in 2026** and **3.2% in 2027** (reference case, assuming the conflict remains limited in duration and scope) — a downgrade from the January 2026 update.
  - Source: https://www.imf.org/en/publications/weo/issues/2026/04/14/world-economic-outlook-april-2026 ; https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/4/14/imf-cuts-global-growth-forecast-during-hormuz-blockade
- **Global headline inflation**: rises to **4.4% in 2026** (reference case) before resuming decline in 2027; slowdown and inflation hit are most pronounced in emerging market and developing economies.
- **Scenarios**: adverse scenario — growth **2.5%**, inflation **5.4%** in 2026; severe scenario — growth **~2.0%** in 2026 and 2027, inflation **>6%**.
  - Source: https://www.imf.org/en/blogs/articles/2026/04/14/war-darkens-global-economic-outlook-and-reshapes-policy-priorities
  - Constraint note: The IMF's own reference/adverse/severe fork (3.1/2.5/2.0% growth) gives the scenario document a ready-made calibration for its 2026–27 opening years; note the WEO was written mid-blockade, before the June memorandum.

### World Bank Global Economic Prospects, June 2026 (published ~11 Jun 2026)
- **Global growth**: forecast to slow to **2.5% in 2026** (from 2.9% in 2025) — the **lowest since the onset of COVID-19** — recovering to **2.8% in 2027** (still 0.4 pp below the 2010s average). Forecasts for **two-thirds of economies** downgraded vs January. Cause: Middle East conflict, higher energy prices, steeper inflation, higher borrowing costs.
- Regional: South Asia strongest region but slowing **7.0% (2025) → 6.3% (2026)**; Middle East/North Africa/Afghanistan/Pakistan **1.6% in 2026 → 5.0% in 2027** (rebound); Sub-Saharan Africa **4.0% (2026) → 4.4% (2027)**. By 2028, developing economies ex-China/India will have had ~a decade of no convergence with advanced-economy incomes.
  - Source: https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/06/11/global-economic-prospects-june-2026-press-release ; https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/2b672b3b0415d6b66c45b66579db4ef5-0050012026/original/GEP-Jun-2026.pdf
  - Constraint note: The World Bank's 2.5% (market-exchange-rate basis, vs IMF's PPP 3.1%) fixes 2026 as a global stagnation year and stalled EM convergence as the starting trend line for the 30-year horizon.

### Oil in 2026: spike, then glut
- **Spike**: Brent crossed **$100/bbl on 8 Mar 2026** (first time in 4 years). Peak figures vary by measure: Brent futures peaked near **$118–126/bbl**; North Sea Dated physical near **$130/bbl**; some physical cargoes reportedly as high as **$150/bbl**. March 2026 was the **largest-ever monthly increase in oil prices** — faster than the Ukraine war, Gulf War, or Iraq War moves.
  - Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_crisis ; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/21/oil-price-iran-war-middle-east.html ; https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=67424
  - Note: because peak prints differ across sources ($118–150 depending on futures vs physical), cite a range, not a single number.
- **Current prices (early July 2026)**: **WTI ~$68.86/bbl, Brent ~$72.11/bbl** — Brent trading near its lowest since late February, i.e., the entire war premium has round-tripped.
  - Source: https://www.forbes.com/advisor/investing/oil-prices-today/ ; https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/brent-crude-oil
- **Glut dynamics**: Hormuz tanker traffic normalizing; Saudi exports approaching pre-war levels; **OPEC+ approved a quota increase of 188,000 b/d for next month**, continuing progressive unwinding of production curbs; market worried about supply glut. Notably, **the UAE exited OPEC during the war** and has fully restored shipping flows.
  - Source: https://fortune.com/article/price-of-oil-06-22-2026/ ; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/19/oil-prices-wti-brent-crude-us-iran-deal-strait-hormuz-shipping-recovery.html
  - Constraint note: H2 2026 starts with oil back at ~$70 and a glut narrative despite the war — the scenario must reconcile a demonstrated $100+ geopolitical risk premium with structural oversupply, and carry the UAE's OPEC exit as a standing fracture in the cartel.
- **UNVERIFIED — full-year OPEC+ quota path**: I confirmed the ~188 kb/d increase approved in early July and the general "progressive unwinding" framing, but did not verify the month-by-month 2026 quota decisions (e.g., what OPEC+ did during the March–April closure). The OPEC MOMR (https://www.opec.org/monthly-oil-market-report.html) is the primary source for backfill.

## Topic 3 — The Warsh Fed

### Confirmation and takeover
- Trump nominated **Kevin Warsh** as 17th Fed chair in **January 2026**; the Senate confirmed him **13 May 2026 by 54–45** — described as the most divisive Fed-chair confirmation vote in history. He took office when Jerome Powell's chair term expired **15 May 2026** (Trump declined to renew Powell).
  - Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/13/kevin-warsh-wins-senate-confirmation-as-the-next-federal-reserve-chair.html ; https://www.npr.org/2026/05/13/nx-s1-5816235/kevin-warsh-federal-reserve-chair-jerome-powell
  - Constraint note: A 54–45 confirmation means the Fed's institutional legitimacy is politically contested at the scenario's start — central-bank-independence erosion is a live 30-year variable, not a hypothetical.

### Policy stance and the hikes-vs-cuts standoff
- **First FOMC meeting as chair, 16–17 Jun 2026**: rates held at **3.50–3.75%** (effective fed funds ~3.63%). But the meeting **signaled higher rates ahead**: nine Fed officials supported higher rates this year, six of them supporting **two quarter-point hikes** — with inflation at a **three-year high of 4.2% y/y** (war-driven energy pass-through).
  - Source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260617a.htm ; https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/17/business/live-news/federal-reserve-interest-rate-kevin-warsh ; https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DFEDTARU
- Warsh, expected by Trump to cut, has instead turned **hawkish**: at his first press conference he stressed returning inflation to the 2% target ("we've all looked around, and we've seen that prices are too high"). He is also forming **five task forces** on Fed communications, data sources, and inflation frameworks — an institutional overhaul.
  - Source: https://www.pbs.org/newshour/economy/watch-live-new-fed-chair-kevin-warsh-holds-first-news-conference-after-interest-rate-decision ; https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/article/kevin-warsh-confirmed-new-fed-chair-as-inflation-kicks-higher-complicating-the-central-banks-path-164303609.html
- **White House friction**: Trump continues to call publicly for cuts ("We need low interest rates. Low interest rates will solve everything"), and the FT reported Trump "piles pressure on Warsh with call for rate cut"; but by **26 Jun 2026** CNBC reported Trump was **easing pressure** on Warsh as inflation topped 4%, saying he has "confidence and faith" in Warsh and will let him make decisions he didn't entrust to Powell. Several top Trump economic advisers have stopped calling for near-term cuts.
  - Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/trump-warsh-fed-rate-cuts-inflation.html ; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/15/trump-warsh-fed-reshape.html
  - Constraint note: The standoff so far resolves toward Warsh's hawkishness with Trump's grudging tolerance — but the scenario should treat "Trump patience with a hiking Fed in a slowing economy" as an unstable equilibrium heading into the 2026 midterms.
  - **UNVERIFIED — specific Truth Social posts**: I found reporting of Trump's public rate-cut calls but could not verify any specific Truth Social post text attacking Warsh; do not quote one.

### June 2026 payrolls (released 2 Jul 2026) and market reaction
- Nonfarm payrolls **+57,000** in June — roughly **half the ~110k consensus**. Unemployment rate ticked down to **4.2%** (from 4.3%) — but due to labor-force exit, not hiring. Leisure & hospitality **–61,000** (despite World Cup group-stage matches in the US); professional & business services **+36,000**. Wage growth ~**3.5% y/y**, above the Fed's comfort zone.
  - Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/02/jobs-report-june-2026-.html ; https://moneywise.com/news/economy/june-2026-jobs-report-57000-payrolls-unemployment-rate-fed-kevin-warsh
- **Market reaction**: per CME FedWatch, odds of one-or-more **hikes by September fell from ~65% before the report to ~50%** in the minutes after; commentary ("quiets the rate-hike conversation") says the print makes 2026 hikes much less likely. Warsh called the jobs picture "steady" and kept emphasizing the 2% target.
  - Source: https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/economy/jobs-report-june-2026-what-to-expect ; https://easternherald.com/2026/07/02/us-jobs-report-nonfarm-payrolls-fed-warsh/
  - Constraint note: The scenario opens with a textbook stagflation trap — 4.2% inflation, 57k payrolls, a hawkish new Fed chair, markets pricing a coin-flip on hikes — so the H2 2026 macro branch is hike-into-weakness vs blink-and-cut, and either path shapes the credibility baseline for the following decades.
- **UNVERIFIED — equity/bond market levels**: I did not pull S&P 500, 10-year Treasury, or dollar levels as of 7 Jul 2026; the FedWatch odds shift is the only market-pricing datum verified here.
