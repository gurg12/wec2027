## 1. Biotech / Longevity

### GLP-1 population effects
- ~18% of US adults using a GLP-1 as of spring 2026, up from ~14% in 2025 (broad definition, all indications). KFF poll: ~1 in 8 (12-12.4%) taking specifically for weight loss/diabetes in 2025; RAND/survey shows weight-loss-specific use rose from 5.8% (Feb 2024) to 12.4% (late 2025). Source: https://www.forbes.com/health/weight-loss/glp-1-statistics/ ; https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/poll-1-in-8-adults-say-they-are-currently-taking-a-glp-1-drug-for-weight-loss-diabetes-or-another-condition-even-as-half-say-the-drugs-are-difficult-to-afford/
- Obesity-rate inflection: US adult obesity ~37% in 2026, down from peak 39.9% ~3 years earlier (Gallup, via NPR). Largest drop in 50-64 cohort (highest usage, ~22%). Projection still shows 47% obese by 2035 absent broader access. Source: https://www.npr.org/sections/shots-health-news/2025/10/28/nx-s1-5587805/glp-1-ozempic-zepbound-gallup-obesity-rate
- Oral GLP-1: first oral GLP-1 FDA-approved end of 2025; a second oral product expected ~April 2026. Expands market to injection-averse users + seniors gaining access. Source: https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/global-research/current-events/obesity-drugs
- Constraint note: A large, growing fraction of the adult population (~1 in 6) is already on metabolic drugs with a measurable population obesity decline — a demand-shock/behavioral variable (food, cardio-metabolic disease, healthcare spend) that compounds over 30 years, not a hypothetical.

### Senolytics & partial epigenetic reprogramming
- Partial reprogramming reached the clinic for the first time Q1 2026 (Life Biosciences' ER-100). At least 4 companies expected in/near Phase I by 2026 — first direct human test of aging-reversal hypothesis. Source: https://beyondtmrw.org/article/senolytics-and-partial-reprogramming-human-trial-status-in-2026
- Altos Labs: >$3B funded, tissue-specific reprogramming portfolio; hired Joan Mannick as CMO 2025 (trial-prep signal). NewLimit: T-cell/immune reprogramming, reported "close" to clinic-ready by Dec 2025. Retro Bio: OpenAI partnership (Aug 2025, claimed 50x reprogramming efficiency); started first human trial with RTR242 (autophagy booster) for Alzheimer's. Source: https://beyondtmrw.org/article/senolytics-and-partial-reprogramming-human-trial-status-in-2026 ; https://www.fightaging.org/archives/2026/06/a-great-deal-more-funding-for-the-development-of-partial-reprogramming-therapies/
- Senolytics setback: Unity Biotechnology (flagship senolytics company) shuttered September 2025. Source: https://beyondtmrw.org/article/senolytics-and-partial-reprogramming-human-trial-status-in-2026
- Constraint note: Aging-reversal is at Phase-0/I as of 2026 — earliest possible efficacy readouts land early in the 30-year window; treat human lifespan extension as an open experiment, not a delivered capability, for at least the first decade.

### AI-designed drugs in clinical trials
- Isomorphic Labs: committed to Phase 1 with first AI-designed candidate by end of 2026; first IND filing expected end-2026; no patients dosed yet (start slipped from late-2025 to late-2026). Raised $2.1B Series B (May 2025/2026). Source: https://www.clinicaltrialsarena.com/news/isomorphic-labs-prepares-trials-ai-designed-drugs/
- Insilico Medicine: rentosertib (IPF), first fully generative-AI-designed drug in Phase II, already dosing patients. Eli Lilly–Insilico deal up to $2.75B (March 2026), largest AI-era pharma deal. Source: https://insilico.com/phase1 ; https://aimmediahouse.com/ai-lifesciences/2026-is-the-year-ai-drug-discovery-meets-clinical-reality
- Industry: >173 AI-discovered drug programs in clinical development; 15-20 expected to reach pivotal Phase III in 2026; zero FDA approvals of an AI-designed drug yet. Source: https://aimmediahouse.com/ai-lifesciences/2026-is-the-year-ai-drug-discovery-meets-clinical-reality
- Constraint note: AI drug discovery is validated at discovery/Phase-II stage but has produced no approvals by 2026 — the 30-year window will see the first approvals and thus the empirical test of whether AI compresses the ~10-15yr, ~90%-attrition drug pipeline.

### BCI state of the art
- Neuralink: ~21 people implanted across 4 countries (US, UK, Canada, UAE) by early 2026; PRIME feasibility study expanding toward 30 before pivotal trial. Source: https://bciintel.com/bci-implant-count/ ; https://nextwavesinsight.com/bci-neuralink-synchron-clinical-trials-2026/
- Synchron: ~10 Stentrode patients (4 Australia, 6 US); completed US feasibility, preparing pivotal for FDA PMA. Precision Neuroscience: holds FDA 510(k) clearance for temporary (≤30-day) implant. Source: https://nextwavesinsight.com/bci-neuralink-synchron-clinical-trials-2026/
- ~100 humans total have received intracortical BCI implants as of 2026. Source: https://bciintel.com/bci-implant-count/
- China's BCI programs: UNVERIFIED — search did not surface hard specifics on China's BCI patient counts/programs (China has state-backed BCI efforts, e.g. NEO/Neucyber and a 2025 invasive-implant milestone reported, but no confirmed count found in this pass). Flag for a dedicated follow-up search.
- Constraint note: BCI is at low-tens-of-patients feasibility scale in 2026 — mass consumer adoption is a late-window (2030s-40s) possibility, not a near-term base case; regulatory pivotal trials are the near-term gate.

## 2. Climate

### Current warming level
- Jan-Nov 2025 global anomaly: +0.60°C vs 1991-2020, = +1.48°C vs 1850-1900 preindustrial. 2025 finishing 2nd- or 3rd-warmest on record (2024 warmest). Source: https://climate.copernicus.eu/copernicus-2025-course-be-joint-second-warmest-year-november-third-warmest-record
- 2023-2025 three-year average on track to exceed +1.5°C for the first time (temporary breach of Paris goal). Nov 2025 alone was +1.54°C; 28 months in ERA5 record have surpassed 1.5°C. Source: https://www.euronews.com/green/2025/12/09/temperature-average-for-2023-2025-on-track-to-exceed-15c-for-first-time-copernicus-data-re
- Constraint note: Baseline warming is ~1.4-1.5°C at window start; the scenario opens with the 1.5°C threshold effectively at/through breach, so 30-yr trajectories realistically span ~1.8-2.7°C — 1.5°C stabilization is off the table.

### Emissions trajectory
- China CO2 flat or falling for ~21 months (since ~March 2024) — longest such run not driven by economic slowdown; Q4 2025 emissions -1% YoY. Driven by solar (+43% YoY output), wind (+14%), nuclear (+8%), coal generation -1.9%. Suggests China's emissions peak may be at/near hand. Source: https://www.carbonbrief.org/analysis-chinas-co2-emissions-have-now-been-flat-or-falling-for-21-months/
- Global still rising: Global Carbon Budget projects +1.1% fossil CO2 in 2025 (aviation, shipping, developing economies). Energy-related CO2 hit record 37.8 Gt in 2024 (+0.8%). Global has NOT plateaued even as China has. Source: https://www.iea.org/reports/global-energy-review-2025/co2-emissions
- Remaining 1.5°C budget from 2025: ~215 GtCO2, ~5 years at 2024 rates. Source: https://www.carbonbrief.org/analysis-chinas-co2-emissions-have-now-been-flat-or-falling-for-21-months/
- Constraint note: The single biggest emitter has likely peaked, but global emissions are still at record highs — the 30-yr window is where the global peak-and-decline either happens or doesn't; the 1.5°C carbon budget is exhausted within the first ~5 years.

### Adaptation spending
- Adaptation finance needs in developing countries: US$310B/yr by 2035 (modelled) or US$365B/yr (NDC/NAP-extrapolated). International public adaptation flows only US$26B in 2023 (down from $28B in 2022). Gap = US$284-339B/yr; needs are 12-14x current flows. Source: https://www.unep.org/resources/adaptation-gap-report-2025
- Constraint note: Adaptation is chronically underfunded by an order of magnitude entering the window — implies large accumulating climate-damage/adaptation-debt overhang over 30 years, especially in developing economies.

### Geoengineering governance
- UK ARIA "Exploring Climate Cooling" programme: ~£57M (commonly cited as £50M) across 21 projects; ~5 involve small-scale outdoor experiments. Marine Cloud Brightening: 3 grants totalling £9.1M (up to £14.1M w/ matched funds). Stratospheric outdoor experiment: £5.5M to Cambridge (Hugh Hunt) — balloon material-aging test, no material release. Source: https://srm360.org/news-reaction/aria-45m-funding-21-climate-cooling-research-projects/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exploring_Climate_Cooling_Programme
- Governance: no binding global moratorium found; ARIA safeguards = no toxic releases, published environmental impact assessments, community consultation. Opposition organized (Solar Geoengineering Non-Use Agreement / non-use letter camp). Source: https://www.solargeoeng.org/arias-exploring-climate-cooling-folly/
- Constraint note: SRM has moved from taboo to state-funded outdoor research by 2026 (UK leading) but at tiny scale with no governance regime — deployment is a plausible but ungoverned wild-card within the 30-yr window.

## 3. Space

### Artemis vs China lunar timeline
- Artemis II (first crewed lunar flyby in 50+ years) launched 1 April 2026, ~10-day free-return mission (Wiseman, Glover, Koch, Hansen) after Feb/March scrubs. Artemis III crewed landing follows (dependent on Starship HLS + suits; historically slipping). Source: https://www.nasa.gov/mission/artemis-ii/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artemis_II
- China: crewed lunar landing targeted 2029-2030; on-track per CNSA (Oct 30 2025); Mengzhou crew vehicle + Lanyue lander on two Long March 10 launches; uncrewed rehearsal 2028-2029. Real possibility China lands before/around Artemis III. Source: https://www.rand.org/pubs/commentary/2025/11/china-is-going-to-the-moon-by-2030-heres-whats-known.html ; https://www.scmp.com/news/china/science/article/3307624/chinas-moon-shot-2030-crewed-lunar-mission-tests-pace-space-agency-says
- Constraint note: A US-China lunar "race" resolves within the first ~4 years of the window (~2028-2030); first-to-return-humans is genuinely contested.

### Jared Isaacman (NASA Administrator)
- Confirmed by Senate 17 Dec 2025 as 15th NASA Administrator; serving as of 2026 (official workforce message dated 22 May 2026). Source: https://spaceflightnow.com/2025/12/18/senate-confirms-jared-isaacman-as-15th-nasa-administrator/ ; https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/workforce-updates/2026/05/22/a-message-from-administrator-jared-isaacman/
- Constraint note: NASA has stable, commercial-space-aligned leadership entering the window — points to continued commercial/Starship-dependent architecture.

### SpaceX Starship
- Flight 12 launched 22 May 2026 — first test of Starship V3 architecture. SpaceX targeting orbital payload delivery in H2 2026 and a first Florida (LC-39A) launch by end-2026. Cadence still ramping, not yet operational. Source: https://spacenews.com/starship-underpins-spacexs-growth-ambitions/ ; https://keeptrack.space/x-report/spacex-brief-2026-07-05
- Constraint note: Starship is mid-development (V3 flight tests, no operational orbital cadence) in 2026 — Artemis III, Mars ambitions, and megaconstellation economics all hinge on it maturing early in the window.

### Tianwen-2 (asteroid sample return)
- Launched 28 May 2025; ~1B km / ~400 days to reach quasi-moon 469219 Kamoʻoalewa; rendezvous ~7 June 2026, CNSA announced arrival + first image 6 July 2026. Sample collection then extended mapping to ~April 2027; sample return capsule to Earth ~late Nov 2027. Source: https://spacenews.com/tianwen-2-arrives-at-asteroid-kamooalewa-first-image-revealed/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tianwen-2
- Constraint note: Confirms China's deep-space sample-return cadence — reinforces China as a full-spectrum space power at window start.

### Commercial space stations
- Vast Haven-1: completed cleanroom integration; launch NET Q1 2027 on Falcon 9 (first commercial station). Axiom: Thales Alenia welding first module; free-flying assembly as early as 2028. NASA awarded new Private Astronaut Missions to both (early 2026), launching ~2027. ISS retirement ~2030 is the forcing function. Source: https://www.nasaspaceflight.com/2026/02/vast-axiom-2026-pam/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vast_(company)
- Constraint note: The LEO commercial-station transition (post-ISS) plays out in the first ~5 years of the window; a gap or a handoff both plausible.

## 4. Historical Base Rates

### Stagflation / interregnum duration
- US Great Inflation: ~1965-1982 (~17 yrs); the acute stagflation ~1968-1982 (~14 yrs). Inflation >6% for a full decade, peaks ~11% (1974), ~13-14.5% (1980). S&P 500 flat 1968-1982 in nominal terms. Source: https://www.federalreservehistory.org/essays/great-inflation ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stagflation
- Resolution: Volcker Shock — fed funds ~11% (1979) to ~20% (June 1981); inflation to <4% by 1983 at cost of ~10% unemployment and 1980-82 recession. Source: https://www.federalreservehistory.org/essays/great-inflation
- 1890s depression: ~1893-1897 (Panic of 1893), ~4-5 yrs. UNVERIFIED precise dating this pass (from general knowledge; not separately searched). Interwar/Great Depression 1929-~1939 (~10 yrs, US recovery to 1929 output ~1936-39). UNVERIFIED this pass.
- Constraint note: Comparable "malaise/interregnum" episodes run ~4-17 years and typically require a decisive policy regime-break to end — a scenario positing a multi-year stagflationary interregnum has strong historical precedent for both its length and its abrupt policy-driven resolution.

### Technology diffusion lags (Solow paradox)
- David (1990), "The Dynamo and the Computer": electric dynamo commercial from ~1880s, but factory electrification diffusion (lighting/motors) hit ~50% and showed TFP impact only in the 1920s — a ~30-40 year lag. Cause = organizational complements (unit drive, factory redesign), not raw tech; capital was only replaced when existing plant wore out. Source: http://digamo.free.fr/david90.pdf
- Constraint note: A ~20-40 year gap between a general-purpose technology's arrival and its measured productivity payoff is the historical norm — directly bounds how fast AI/biotech GPTs can be expected to show up in TFP within a 30-yr window (payoff likely back-loaded).

### Sovereign debt at high debt/GDP
- Reinhart-Rogoff "Growth in a Time of Debt" (2010): dataset of 44 countries, ~200 yrs, 3,700+ observations; claimed median growth ~-1pp and mean growth sharply lower above 90% debt/GDP. Source: https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w15639/w15639.pdf
- Heavily contested: Herndon-Ash-Pollin found coding/spreadsheet errors + selective data; corrected figure ~+2.2% average growth above 90%, not -0.1%. No consensus threshold; "110%" is not a distinct RR analytical break. Source: https://www.epi.org/blog/reinhart-rogoff-magical-90-percent-threshold/
- Constraint note: High debt/GDP correlates with slower growth but the sharp "cliff" threshold is discredited — a scenario should treat >100-110% debt as a drag/vulnerability multiplier, not a deterministic crisis trigger. UNVERIFIED: a clean base-rate "probability of sovereign crisis per year at >110%" figure was not isolated this pass.

### Great-power war under nuclear deterrence
- "Long Peace": zero direct great-power wars since 1945 (~80 yrs); term coined by John Lewis Gaddis (1986). Widely attributed to nuclear deterrence + bipolarity, though contested (war decline predates nukes). Source: https://www.defensepriorities.org/explainers/the-restraining-effect-of-nuclear-deterrence/ ; https://grokipedia.com/page/Long_Peace
- One cited constant-risk model gives only ~21% chance of a peace this long by chance — i.e. the streak is statistically surprising and not obviously "safe." Source: https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/aSzxoj7irC5jNHceB/how-likely-is-world-war-iii
- Constraint note: 80-year base rate of zero direct great-power war is the anchor for the "no WW3" prior over the next 30 yrs, but the deterrence explanation is contested and the streak may be partly luck — a scenario is justified in assigning low-but-nonzero great-power-war probability, not ~0.

### Frequency of >$100bn cyber events
- Largest single cyberattack to date: NotPetya (2017), ~$10B global damage (individual-firm hits: Merck ~$870M, FedEx/TNT ~$400M, Maersk, Mondelez ~$188M). No confirmed single cyber event has reached $100B. Source: https://cyberranges.com/how-did-notpetya-cost-businesses-over-10-billion-in-damages/ ; https://www.brookings.edu/articles/how-the-notpetya-attack-is-reshaping-cyber-insurance/
- Aggregate (not single-event) cyber cost estimated ~$354B in 2025 across all attacks. A single >$100B event has not yet occurred (tail risk modeled but unrealized). Source: https://nordvpn.com/cybersecurity/statistics/
- Constraint note: A single >$100B cyber event is UNVERIFIED as a historical base rate — it has zero occurrences to date (max ~$10B), so in-window it is a genuine tail/first-of-kind risk rather than a recurring event with an empirical frequency.
