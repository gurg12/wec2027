# AI Grounding Dossier — as of 2026-07-07

Raw research notes for the "as of July 2026" scenario document. All items web-verified 2026-07-07 unless marked UNVERIFIED. Reliability caveats noted inline; several frontier-model details come from aggregator/blog sources rather than lab primary sources.

## Topic 1: AI capability frontier as of mid-2026

### 1.1 Frontier model landscape (July 2026)

- **US frontier lineup (approximate, per aggregator leaderboards):** Claude Opus 4.8 (Anthropic) cited as overall leader; GPT-5.5 (OpenAI), Gemini 3.1 Pro (Google), Grok 4.3 (xAI) complete the frontier tier. GPT-5.5 Pro: 39.6% on FrontierMath Tier 4 (vs Opus 4.8 Thinking 22.9%); Gemini 3.1 Pro: 94.3% GPQA Diamond, 44.4% Humanity's Last Exam, 77.1% ARC-AGI-2. Source: https://felloai.com/best-ai-models/ ; https://lmcouncil.ai/benchmarks (aggregator sites — treat exact version numbers as lower-confidence; primary lab pages not fetched).
- **Very recent / imminent releases (late June–July 2026):** Anthropic Claude Sonnet 5 (June 30, 63.2% SWE-bench Pro, $2/$10 intro pricing); "Claude Fable 5" redeployed July 1 after ~3-week outage (described as first "Mythos-class" model); OpenAI GPT-5.6 family in gated preview since June 26; Grok 4.5 in private beta June 28; Gemini 3.5 Pro slated for July launch. Source: https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates ; https://felloai.com/best-ai-models/ — **UNVERIFIED in detail** (aggregator-only sourcing; the "Fable 5 outage/redeploy" claim in particular found only on secondary sites).
- **Chinese open-weight frontier — GLM-5.2 (Zhipu / Z.ai):** released June 13, 2026; open weights (MIT) published third week of June. ~750B-param sparse MoE, ~40B active, 1M-token context, "IndexShare" sparse attention. Strongest open model: 81.0 Terminal-Bench 2.1, 62.1 SWE-bench Pro; Artificial-Analysis-style Intelligence Index v4.1 score 51 vs MiniMax-M3 44, DeepSeek V4 Pro 44, Kimi K2.6 43. Pricing ~$1.40/$4.40 per M tokens vs GPT-5.5 at $5/$30 and Claude Opus $5/$25 (~1/6 the cost). Sources: https://www.eigent.ai/blog/glm-5-2 ; https://huggingface.co/blog/mlabonne/glm-5 ; https://www.trendingtopics.eu/glm-5-2-chinas-zhipu-ai-beats-even-googles-top-models-with-its-new-open-llm/ ; https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-glm-gpt-glm-52-passes-vibe
- Other Chinese entrants current as of mid-2026: DeepSeek V4 Pro, Kimi K2.6 (Moonshot), MiniMax-M3 — all within ~7 index points of GLM-5.2; Zhipu reportedly forecasting an "Open Fable"-class model by end of year. Source: https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-glm-gpt-glm-52-passes-vibe
- **Constraint note:** By H2 2026 the open-weight/closed gap is roughly a single model generation and Chinese open models undercut US frontier pricing ~6x — any 30-year scenario must start from a multipolar, rapidly-commoditizing frontier, not a durable US-lab monopoly.

### 1.2 Agentic coding benchmarks vs real-world performance

- **METR task-length horizon:** doubling time compressed from ~7 months (2019–2024) to ~4 months (2024-on); some estimates as fast as 89 days. May 2026 METR Frontier Risk Report (Feb–Mar 2026 pilot with Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI): strongest assessed agents ~16–20 hours at 50%-reliability horizon, 3–4 hours at 80% — near/beyond the reliable measurement ceiling of the Time Horizon 1.1 suite (estimates >16h flagged unreliable due to suite saturation). Sources: https://theaidigest.org/time-horizons ; https://ai2027-tracker.com/predictions/metr-doubling/ ; https://metr.org/
- **SWE-bench saturation:** SWE-bench Verified went 13.8% (Devin, Mar 2024) → 82.0% (Claude Sonnet 4.5, Sep 2025) and is treated as saturated; harder successor SWE-bench Pro (Scale AI, 1,865 held-out multi-step tasks) had top frontier models at ~23% at launch, with mid-2026 leaders at ~62–63% (GLM-5.2 62.1, Claude Sonnet 5 63.2). Sources: https://www.codesota.com/agentic ; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.14499
- **Benchmark–production gap:** practitioner analyses (e.g. Apr 2026) document persistent gaps between SWE-bench scores and production agentic coding reliability (long-horizon reliability, memory, monitoring remain weak). Source: https://tianpan.co/blog/2026-04-09-agentic-coding-production-swebench-gap
- **Constraint note:** Capability curves (horizon doubling ~4 months) are outrunning both measurement instruments and enterprise reliability — scenarios should not assume benchmark performance translates 1:1 into deployed economic capability in 2026–2028.

- Contract amendments beginning Oct 2025 removed Microsoft's prohibition on independently pursuing AGI; restructured relationship confirmed publicly around Build 2026 (June 2026). Sources: https://www.theregister.com/2026/04/27/microsofts_and_openai_change_relationship/ ; https://venturebeat.com/technology/microsoft-ai-chief-says-company-was-set-free-from-openai-to-pursue-superintelligence
- MAI Superintelligence Team announced by Mustafa Suleyman Nov 6, 2025 ("humanist superintelligence"); Suleyman separated from Copilot day-to-day in Mar 2026 to focus on MAI. First MAI proprietary models shipped Apr 2, 2026 (MAI-Transcribe-1, MAI-Voice-1, MAI-Image-2); ~7 MAI models announced by Build 2026; frontier-class general LLM targeted for 2027. Sources: https://www.forbes.com/sites/janakirammsv/2026/04/02/microsoft-builds-its-own-ai-model-stack-to-reduce-openai-dependence/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_AI
- Related: Microsoft took over the Stargate Norway data center commitment as OpenAI pulled back (Apr 2026). Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/15/openai-stargate-norway-project-microsoft.html
- **Constraint note:** The Microsoft–OpenAI decoupling means scenario paths should treat "hyperscaler-captive lab" as a dissolving structure — by 2027+ Microsoft is a frontier lab competitor, and OpenAI's compute is multi-sourced.

### 1.4 Enterprise AI adoption (2026)

- 88% of enterprises use AI in some form, but only ~23% have scaled AI agents and ~12% of CEOs report combined revenue gain + cost reduction; ~79% adopted agents in some form but only ~11–31% run agents in production (estimates vary by survey). Sources: https://writer.com/blog/enterprise-ai-adoption-2026/ ; https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agent-adoption-2026-enterprise-data-points ; https://azumo.com/artificial-intelligence/ai-insights/enterprise-ai-adoption-statistics
- Agent ROI: median payback 5.1 months where it works, but only 41% of rollouts reach positive ROI within 12 months and 19% never reach payback; Gartner (2025) forecast >40% of agentic AI projects cancelled by 2027. Financial services leads (47% of banking/insurance orgs with agents in production). Sources: https://www.saasultra.com/ai-agent-statistics-adoption-roi-industries/ ; https://www.accelirate.com/agentic-ai-statistics-2026/
- Note: these are secondary stat-aggregator compilations of Deloitte/McKinsey/Gartner surveys; individual figures are directionally consistent but not independently verified against the primary reports — treat specific percentages as ±. Primary anchor: Deloitte State of AI in the Enterprise 2026: https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/what-we-do/capabilities/applied-artificial-intelligence/content/state-of-ai-in-the-enterprise.html
- **Constraint note:** As of H2 2026 there is a wide adoption-to-value gap (near-universal usage, ~10–25% scaled/ROI-positive) — the scenario's early years must price in a multi-year diffusion lag between frontier capability and realized productivity.

### 1.5 AI capex (2025 actual, 2026 guidance, pipeline)

- **Big-4 hyperscaler capex:** ~$410B actual in 2025 → ~$690–725B guided for 2026 (+~77%). Company-level 2026: Amazon ~$200B, Microsoft ~$190B, Google ~$175–185B, Meta ~$115–145B (Meta raised guidance citing component/DC cost inflation). Analysts project big-tech capex tops $1T in 2027. Sources: https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/big-tech/big-techs-ai-spending-plans-reach-725-billion ; https://futurumgroup.com/insights/ai-capex-2026-the-690b-infrastructure-sprint/ ; https://valueaddvc.com/ai-spending
- Supporting datapoints: Microsoft FQ3 capex $30.9B (+84% y/y), AI revenue run-rate >$37B; Alphabet Q1-2026 capex $35.7B (2x y/y), Google Cloud backlog >$460B. Source: https://futurumgroup.com/insights/ai-capex-2026-the-690b-infrastructure-sprint/
- **OpenAI / Stargate:** Stargate JV (OpenAI+SoftBank+Oracle, announced Jan 2025) — $500B / 10 GW nameplate target, five additional sites added Sep 2025. Altman cited ~$1.4T in aggregate 8-year compute commitments in late 2025; **in Feb 2026 OpenAI cut its through-2030 compute spend target to ~$600B (-57%)** and shifted toward renting rather than owning capacity; WSJ-reported 2026 revenue miss cited as driver. Sources: https://openai.com/index/announcing-the-stargate-project/ ; https://www.techtimes.com/articles/316807/20260519/openai-cut-stargates-spending-pledge-14-trillion-600-billion-now-renting-what-it-vowed-build.htm ; https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/analysis/openai-building-stargate-nvidia-oracle-chatgpt/ — the $1.4T→$600B cut is reported by secondary outlets; **UNVERIFIED against an OpenAI primary statement**.
- **Total US data-center pipeline dollars: UNVERIFIED as a single figure.** Found instead: big-4 2026 capex ~$725B; Stargate $500B nameplate; $130B of projects blocked/stalled in Q1 2026 alone (see 2.4) — implying a multi-trillion multi-year announced pipeline, but no authoritative aggregate located.
- **Constraint note:** 2026 opens with capex still accelerating (~+77% y/y) but the first major demand-side retrenchment (OpenAI -57%) already on record — scenarios should allow for a capex digestion/correction phase in 2026–2028 without assuming the buildout stops.

## Topic 2: AI public opinion and backlash

### 2.1 Stanford AI Index 2026 — expert vs public gap

- 2026 AI Index (released April 2026): 73% of AI experts expect positive impact on how people do their jobs vs 23% of the public — **a 50-point gap** (confirms the reported figure). Similar gaps: economy 69% vs 21%; medical care 84% vs 44%. Source: https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/public-opinion ; https://www.kqed.org/news/12079472/stanford-study-ai-experts-are-optimistic-about-ai-the-rest-of-us-not-so-much
- Accompanying Ipsos 31-country survey: 52% globally nervous about AI products/services, +12 pts vs prior wave. Experts expect genAI to assist 18% of US work hours by 2030 vs public estimate of 10%. Sources: https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report ; https://kineangst.de/en/news/ki-skepsis-waechst-weltweit-deutschland-positiver
- **Constraint note:** Elite/public divergence of ~50 pts at scenario start is a structural political risk variable — legitimacy, not capability, is the binding constraint in early scenario years.

### 2.2 Gallup — Gen Z attitudes

- Walton Family Foundation / GSV / Gallup survey (fielded Feb 24–Mar 4, 2026; n=1,572, ages 14–29, probability-based Gallup Panel): share of Gen Z saying AI makes them "excited" fell **36% → 22%** year-over-year (confirms reported figure); "hopeful" 27% → 18%; "angry" 22% → 31%; "AI tools help me learn faster" 53% → 46%. Adoption/usage held steady while sentiment fell. Sources: https://news.gallup.com/poll/708224/gen-adoption-steady-skepticism-climbs.aspx ; https://www.axios.com/2026/04/09/ai-gen-z-polling-gallup ; report PDF: https://static.waltonfamilyfoundation.org/73/0c/1a0017594d8eb3c0a667766ae914/wff-gallup-gsv-gen-z-2026-ai-report-final.pdf
- **Constraint note:** The most AI-exposed generation is souring fastest ("more exposure, less excitement") — scenarios cannot assume generational turnover automatically delivers pro-AI publics.

### 2.3 "Too fast" polling (Quinnipiac, Economist/YouGov)

- Economist/YouGov, May 9–11, 2026: **71% of Americans say AI development is moving too fast**; 27% about right; 2% too slow. Bipartisan: Democrats 77%, Independents 69%, Republicans 68%. Pessimists outnumber optimists ~2:1. Source: https://yougov.com/en-us/articles/54762-most-americans-say-artificial-intelligence-ai-development-moving-too-fast-twice-as-many-ai-pessimists-as-ai-optimists-may-9-11-2026-economist-yougov-poll
- Follow-up Economist/YouGov (May 29–Jun 1, 2026): more expect AI to hurt than help the economy. Source: https://yougov.com/en-us/articles/54888-more-expect-artificial-intelligence-ai-affect-economy-negatively-than-positively-americans-split-how-ai-will-impact-their-lives-may-29-june-1-2026-economist-yougov-poll
- Quinnipiac (2026): 55% of US adults think AI will do more harm than good in their day-to-day lives, up from 44% a year earlier; 63% expect AI to reduce job availability; only 35% express trust in the technology. Sources (secondary summaries citing Quinnipiac): https://www.aei.org/op-eds/why-americans-fear-the-ai-future/ ; https://dataconomy.com/2026/05/19/americans-say-ai-advancing-too-fast-regulation/ — exact Quinnipiac release page not fetched; figures **partially UNVERIFIED at primary source**.
- 71% of Americans would oppose a data center in their own area (poll cited in Time coverage). Source: https://time.com/7377579/ai-data-centers-people-movement-cover/
- **Constraint note:** "Too fast" supermajorities that cross party lines make AI regulation a rare bipartisan-salience issue at scenario start — expect policy volatility in both directions through the 2026 midterms and 2028.

### 2.4 Data center cancellations and local opposition (Heatmap Pro Q1 2026)

- Heatmap Pro Q1 2026 report (covered June 2026): **at least 20 data center projects cancelled after local pushback in Q1 2026** — a single-quarter record exceeding all of the prior year (~25 cancellations in full-year 2025) — representing **>$41.7B in investment and ≥3.5 GW of demand**; ~100 new local fights logged in Q1; active opposition groups grew 396 (end-2025) → 833 (end-Mar 2026) across 49 states. Sources: https://heatmap.news/politics/local-opposition-data-center-cancellations ; https://fortune.com/2026/06/16/data-center-opposition-construction-delays-blocks-report/
- Broader tally: ~75 projects worth ~$130B stalled or blocked in Q1 2026 (cancelled + delayed + blocked). Sources: https://thenextweb.com/news/data-center-opposition-75-projects-blocked-q1-2026 ; https://time.com/7377579/ai-data-centers-people-movement-cover/ (note: the $41.7B/20-project figure is cancellations only; $130B/75 includes delays/blocks — don't conflate).
- Local bans passed in Holyoke MA, Monterey Park CA, Seattle WA; grievances: electricity bills, water, noise, land use, tax abatements, distrust of tech billionaires. Sources: https://time.com/7377579/ai-data-centers-people-movement-cover/ ; https://grist.org/politics/data-center-ai-bipartisan-backlash/
- **Constraint note:** Siting/permitting friction is now a first-order constraint on the compute buildout — scenario energy/compute curves should apply a rising local-veto tax on US capacity through the late 2020s.

### 2.5 Anti-AI politics in 2026

- Anti-data-center/anti-AI backlash is explicitly bipartisan (environmental left + populist Trump-aligned right) and is a live 2026 midterm issue; CNBC reports it threatens Pennsylvania GOP incumbents. Sources: https://time.com/7377579/ai-data-centers-people-movement-cover/ ; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/24/ai-data-centers-pennsylvania-republicans-2026-election.html
- Candidates running on it: Francesca Hong (WI state rep, gubernatorial run) co-drafted a statewide data-center moratorium bill (Feb 2026); Alex Bores (NY, congressional run) released an 8-point national AI framework incl. AI job-loss reporting and an "AI dividend"; Arizona enacted a data-center moratorium. Sources: https://time.com/article/2026/06/18/ai-elections-data-center-backlash/ ; https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/2026-election/candidate-center-brewing-midterm-ai-war-unveils-agenda-rcna257735
- **Federal preemption fight:** 10-year state-AI-law preemption stripped from OBBBA by Senate 99-1 (2025); moratorium also omitted from the 2026 NDAA; Trump signed an executive order targeting state AI laws / seeking uniform federal standards ("ONE RULE" push), though the final EO text reportedly does NOT preempt state laws on child safety, compute/data-center infrastructure, or procurement. Sources: https://statescoop.com/state-ai-law-moratorium-omitted-2026-defense-bill-trump-eo/ ; https://www.paulhastings.com/insights/client-alerts/president-trump-signs-executive-order-challenging-state-ai-laws ; https://www.lw.com/en/insights/ai-executive-order-targets-state-laws-and-seeks-uniform-federal-standards ; https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/alerts/2026/03/examining-the-landscape-and-limitations-of-the-federal-push-to-override-state-ai-regulation
- **AI money in the midterms:** pro-AI super-PAC network "Leading the Future" raised >$75M, spent $23.5M across dozens of races; Anthropic contributed $20M to "Public First Action" opposing federal preemption without adequate federal safeguards. Sources: https://www.npr.org/2026/06/22/nx-s1-5856359/ai-anthropic-congress-spending-openai-midterms-election ; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/15/ai-2026-elections-midterms-campaign-donations.html
- **Constraint note:** By H2 2026 AI is a contested electoral issue with organized money on both sides and no settled federal-vs-state framework — a 30-year scenario should treat US AI governance as unresolved and election-sensitive at t=0, with the 2026 midterms as the first branch point.
