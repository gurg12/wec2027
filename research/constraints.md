# Constraints — things the scenario must not contradict

Derived from the Phase 0 dossier (2026-07-07). Every chapter is checked against this page. Where the spine and these facts conflict, the facts win and the spine's *texture* adjusts.

## Fixed initial conditions (H2 2026 opening state)
1. The Iran war ran 28 Feb–Apr 2026 (~900 strikes day one; Ali Khamenei killed; Hormuz largely closed ~13 mb/d); ceasefire 7–8 Apr; Islamabad Memorandum signed 17 Jun; **60-day window closes ~16 Aug 2026** with nuclear program, sanctions, frozen assets unresolved. The truce has already failed once (April collapse) — treat it as fragile, not settled.
2. Mojtaba Khamenei is supreme leader but **has never appeared in public** (reported grave injuries); father's funeral 4–5 Jul. Iran's internal power question is open at t=0.
3. Oil: Brent ~$72 / WTI ~$69 in early July, war premium fully round-tripped; glut narrative; OPEC+ unwinding curbs; **UAE has left OPEC**. Any H2 2026 oil path starts near $70, not $100.
4. Gold ~$4,000–4,100 (not the Jan peak $5,589); silver ~$60. Forecast corridor in print: $4,900 (GS, cut) to $6,300 (JPM 2027). A move to >$6,000 by end-2028 is within published bank forecasts — the spine's 2028 beat is consistent.
5. Fed: Warsh (confirmed 54–45) holding at 3.50–3.75% with CPI 4.2% and payrolls +57k; nine officials leaned hike in June; market ~50/50 on a 2026 hike. **The spine's Sep–Oct "holds despite ~50% hike pricing" matches; do not write a 2026 cut before December.**
6. Ukraine: war ongoing, front static near Pokrovsk; peace track (20-point framework) stalled; EU €90bn committed through 2027; Ukraine holds deep-strike escalation dominance (13–17% simultaneous Russian refining loss — never cite 38–40% as current outage); Russia ~1% growth, NWF liquid buffer nearly gone by end-2026. A 2027 exhaustion ceasefire is consistent; a Russian battlefield victory or 2026 treaty is not.
7. US politics: midterms Nov 2026; AI/data centers already a bipartisan backlash issue with organized money on both sides; federal preemption failed 99–1. Putin is 73; Xi is 73; Trump term ends Jan 2029 — real figures act only within these horizons.

## Quantitative rails (dimensional consistency)
8. Growth: 2026 global growth ~2.5% (WB basis) / 3.1% (IMF PPP); inflation 4.4% global, 4.2% US. The interregnum decade averages ~3% rich-world inflation per the spine — never print a sub-2% US CPI year before the mid-2030s or a >8% non-crisis year.
9. AI: capex 2026 ~$690–725B (Big-4) and still rising into 2027 crash; usage compounding (METR horizon doubling ~4 mo; token growth ~4x through the 2027 drawdown per spine). Chinese open weights ≈ one generation behind, 1/6 price — "90% enterprise parity" by 2027 is consistent.
10. Enterprise adoption gap at t=0: 88% use / ~12% clear ROI. Measured TFP gains must NOT appear before ~2029–30 (electrification lag at 3x speed: GPT-4 2023 + ~7 yrs ≈ 2030). 
11. Robotics: 2025 humanoid shipments ~11k (units mostly spectacle); 2026F ~50k. Spine's ~1M units/yr by 2030 implies ~5 consecutive doublings — aggressive but chartable; keep the curve explicit in the ledger. Waymo 500k rides/wk 2026 → a >50% ride-hail-miles city by 2029 is consistent. Europe's first robotaxi is Zagreb/London, **not Madrid** — spine texture corrected.
12. Demographics: global TFR 2.25 falling through 2.1 ~2034–36; the workers of 2050 are already born. Global population ~8.3bn (2026) — a peak at ~9.2–9.4bn in 2044 requires births to undershoot the UN median hard but stay above IHME-low; frame the 2044 "peak human" announcement as a *provisional statistical recognition*, contested for years (the honest reading of a 2044 peak claim given a UN-median 2084 peak).
13. Energy: solar ~600–665 GW/yr installs and $108/kWh packs at t=0; interconnection queues >5 yrs; data centers 485→950 TWh by 2030. Fusion: nothing material before late 2030s (SPARC plasma ~2027, Q>1 later; ITER ~2034+). SMRs single-digit GW before 2035.
14. Climate: +1.48°C (2025); first calendar year ~1.75°C by 2034 is plausible on current decadal trend (+0.2–0.3°C/decade); "regularly >2.0°C years" by 2047 stays on trajectory toward ~2.2–2.4°C by 2100. China peaked (~2025); global peak ~2035 per spine — consistent with record-but-flattening global emissions now.
15. Biotech: zero AI-designed drug approvals at t=0; first Phase III entries 2026 → first approvals ~2029–31 (spine's 2031 "proof of benefit" cluster is consistent with a ~5-yr Phase III-to-approval lag). Partial reprogramming Phase I now → first aging-pathway approvals ~2036 = fastest-plausible, flag as such. BCI ~100 patients ever at t=0 → ~500k by 2038 is aggressive; keep it medical-only and note the curve.
16. Space: Artemis II flew Apr 2026; US/China crewed-landing race resolves ~2028–30 — the book must pick a winner around then (median: both land, months apart, ~2029–30). Starship still developmental at t=0.

## Base-rate rails
17. Interregnum length: comparable episodes ran 4–17 years ending in a policy regime-break — the spine's 2026–~2035 money arc (fiscal dominance → 2040s re-anchoring) sits inside precedent.
18. Great-power war: zero direct since 1945; the book keeps it that way (Taiwan quarantine 2028 and cyber event 2034 stay sub-war) — justified but flag the ~20% residual tail honestly where the spine says to.
19. A >$100bn cyber event has never happened (max ~$10bn). The 2034 event ($200–400bn) is a first-of-kind tail — the text must present it as unprecedented in scale, not routine.
20. No pandemic-class biological event in the median path — pre-registered omission (Appendix B).

## Naming/People rails
