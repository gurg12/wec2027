## Series to maintain (add row per chapter/year)
- Oil: chokepoint decade → electricity displaces oil as binding commodity ~2030 → structural decline of oil pricing power; nominal drift keeps prices from collapsing to pre-2020 levels.
- Gold: structurally bid through fiscal-dominance era (new highs >$6,000 by end-2028 per spine, inside JPM's published $6,300 2027 corridor); re-anchoring + asteroid-return callback pressure by 2045+.

- CPI: interregnum average ~3%; no sub-2% year before mid-2030s; no >8% non-crisis year.
- Compute: hyperscaler capex $410B (25) → ~$700B (26) → 2027 equity crack WITHOUT usage dip; training compute keeps scaling ~half-order/yr early, slowing to Moore-ish pace as Accords bite (2035+, attested clusters).
- Humanoids: 11k (25) → ~50k (26) → ~1M/yr by 2030 → 10M cumulative by 2039 → eldercare killer app.
- TFR: 2.24 (26) → through 2.1 ~2034 → ~1.8 by mid-40s; global pop peak announced 2044 at ~9.3bn (provisional, contested).
- Warming: +0.2–0.3/decade: ~1.6 (2030), first ~1.75 calendar year 2034, >2.0 years regular by 2047; trajectory ~2.2–2.4 by 2100.
